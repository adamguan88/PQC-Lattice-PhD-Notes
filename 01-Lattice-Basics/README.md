## 1. Definition of a Lattice

Let

$$
B = [b_1,b_2,\ldots,b_n]
$$

be a matrix whose columns are linearly independent vectors.

The lattice generated by $B$ is

$$
\mathcal{L}(B) = \{Bz : z \in \mathbb{Z}^n\}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\mathcal{L}(B)
=
\{z_1b_1 + z_2b_2 + \cdots + z_nb_n : z_i \in \mathbb{Z}\}.
$$

The important point is that the coefficients are integers.

The vectors $b_1,\ldots,b_n$ form a basis of the lattice.
