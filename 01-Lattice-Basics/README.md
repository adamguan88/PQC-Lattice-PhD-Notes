# Lecture 01 — Lattice Basics

## 1. What Is a Lattice?

Let

$$
B = [b_1,b_2,\ldots,b_n]
$$

be a basis consisting of linearly independent vectors.

The lattice generated by $B$ is

$$
\mathcal{L}(B)
=
\left\{
Bz : z \in \mathbb{Z}^n
\right\}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\mathcal{L}(B)
=
\left\{
z_1b_1 + z_2b_2 + \cdots + z_nb_n
:
z_i \in \mathbb{Z}
\right\}.
$$

The important point is that the coefficients are integers.
